## Try it

I built the PHP sales funnels; the data they sent back fed dashboards account managers used
to answer one question for each client: for the money they put in, where did the sales come
from? Pick a date range or traffic source, then open a funnel to see where people drop off
and which sources pay for themselves.

This is a reconstruction from memory with invented clients and numbers. It shows the kind of
view the team relied on, not the original screens.
