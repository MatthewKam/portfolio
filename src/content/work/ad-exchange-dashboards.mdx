## Try it

I built the PHP sales funnels, and the data they sent back fed the dashboards affiliate managers
used to answer one question for each affiliate: for the money going in, where were the sales
coming from? Pick a date range or traffic source, then open an affiliate to see where people
drop off and which sources pay for themselves.

## The problem

Affiliate managers needed to track each affiliate's conversions so they could send more
traffic to the most profitable sources and fulfill orders. The bottleneck was the data itself:
it arrived raw and didn't give them enough insight to make those decisions.

## What I did

I talked with the managers to understand what they needed to know, then worked out how to
show it. Quick-glance metrics came first, backed by deeper analytics that showed trends over
time and put the numbers each manager cared about front and center.

## Result

Managers could spend more of their time finding new affiliates in different verticals, and
they could share key insights with the affiliates already in the network.
