## Referral performance

Merchants need to know whether a program pays for itself. These analytics sat on the referrals
list: referral revenue, referred orders and rewards given, with each month split by whether the
friend bought once or started a subscription. Hover or use the arrow keys on the chart to read
a month.

## Rewards performance

Credit only matters if customers come back to use it. On the rewards list, merchants could
compare what rewards members spend over their first twelve orders against everyone else, and
track credit from issued to redeemed, expired or still owed.

<RewardsAnalytics client:visible />

## The problem

Merchants could create rewards and referral programs and add them to customer journeys in the
canvas view, but once a program was live they couldn't see how it was doing outside of a sales bump. Nothing showed the results visually, so they had no data to make educated decisions with.

## What I did

Working with a designer, I added two things. First, a simpler way to build the pages customers
see: rewards landing pages and referral link pages. Second, analytics on the rewards and
referral list pages, so merchants could see how each program was performing at a glance.

## Result

Merchants could finally see what their programs were doing. Instead of judging a reward or
referral by whether sales bumped, they could check the numbers right from the list, spot which
programs were paying off, and adjust the ones that weren't. The simpler page setup made it
quicker to get a new landing page or referral link live.
