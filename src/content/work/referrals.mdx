## Referral performance

Merchants need to know whether the program pays for itself. This page shows referral revenue,
referred orders and rewards given, with each month split by whether the friend bought once or
started a subscription. Hover or use the arrow keys on the chart to read a month.

<ReferralAnalytics client:visible />

## Rewards performance

Credit only matters if customers come back to use it. This page compares what rewards members
spend over their first twelve orders against everyone else, and tracks credit from issued to
redeemed, expired or still owed.
