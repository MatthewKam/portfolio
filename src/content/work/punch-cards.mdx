This is the card as a customer sees it in their account. Place a few orders, redeem the

## Make it theirs

Merchants style the card from a form beside a live preview: copy, step icons, a banner,
spacing, and whether steps without a reward show their order number. Drag "Orders placed"
to watch the card fill, and switch to Phone to see the narrow layout.
