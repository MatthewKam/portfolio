## Make it theirs

Merchants style the card from a form beside a live preview: copy, reward text and icons, step
icons, a banner, spacing, and whether steps without a reward show their order number. They can
also hide the card from the customer portal entirely. Every change applies to the card in Try
it above, too. Drag "Orders placed" to watch the card fill, and switch to Phone to see the
narrow layout.

## The problem

Punch cards came from three directions at once. Merchants were asking for a simple loyalty
program. Retention mattered too: a card that fills with every order gives subscribers a reason
to stay for the next one. And a competitor already had it. Our CEO saw punch cards as a feature
we were missing, one that could help us beat them and bring in revenue of our own.

## Constraints

The urgency was mostly self-imposed: we felt we were falling behind, and the CEO wanted
something in merchants' hands fast. That set the timeline: one week for the first version.

## What I did

We sat down with product, design and backend to agree on the end goal, what was realistic in a
week, and who would own what, so we used the time well. I built the merchant-facing form, the
experience builder with its live preview, and the integration with the flow canvas, where
merchants add punch cards to their customer journeys.

## The hard part

Building the experience builder from the designs was only half the job. The customer portal
lived in a separate application, so every change a merchant made in the builder had to show
up there exactly as previewed. On top of that came the edge cases we had to talk through and
iron out: refunds, finished punch cards, and customers with more than one card.

## Result

Punch cards became our base rewards flow, with a setting to show or hide the punch card UI for
merchants who didn't want it. Adoption was immediate, and merchants loved it.
